## Surfaces, dialogs and panels

**One card, one benchmark.** Everything card-shaped renders `Components/Card`, and every card carries the
Stat Card design by default — tone glow, accent bar, spring lift, 44px icon tile with pop and wiggle — as
decoration, clickable or not. The only exception is `variant="panel"`, the frame around a Data Table (lifting
it would fight scrolling). The compact Stat Card is the same design at a smaller size. Card also owns focus, selected and
disabled. **Icons: cards right, rows left, dialogs left** (see Introduction). Stat Card, Entity Card and Choice Cards are content layouts *on* Card, not
separate cards; rows in a list use `Item`. Hover Preview is a popover, not a card. The consistency test fails
the build on a hand-drawn card (`bg-card` with a border or radius).

## Design decisions (decided 2026-11-03)

1. **Floating labels**, app-wide: the label rests inside an empty field and floats onto the border on focus or
   once there is a value. (Today's app floats only where a screen opts in; v2 makes it the standard.) — Done:
   every field built from `fieldVariants()` floats (Components/Field → Floating Labels).
2. **Structural headings stay UPPERCASE** — dialog, page, section, settings card, column header, stat caption,
   eyebrow — via the `heading-case` utility (the twin of `HEADING_CASE_SX`). Buttons, input labels, content
   titles and sentences keep their case.
3. **Colours stay admin-configurable.** The seven hues are the preset swatches of the colour picker — the same
   seven names as the live `KIT_SWATCHES` — not fixed assignments; an admin's own hex works everywhere (`custom`).
4. **One chart engine: Recharts** (Components/Chart). Existing charts move over as their screens are rebuilt.
5. **A single value is never a dialog** (standing rule): `OptionPickerDialog` becomes a Combobox; dialogs remain
   for multi-select.
